Deployment
1. Upload index.html and the assets folder to the web root for catalystmediapartners.com.
2. Confirm the domain resolves and HTTPS is active before submitting the URL to a payment processor.
3. Confirm lucas@catalystmediapartners.com receives mail and is listed in the processor account as appropriate.
4. Match the public business details to the website: Catalyst Media Partners Inc, DBA Catalyst Events, business address and phone.
5. Add a hosted payment link only to a clearly described event-services order or issued invoice. The website directs customers to request an order first.
6. Review the general policies against final exhibit, sponsorship, advertising, registration and media-service agreements before production use. Event-specific orders should state when they supersede the website summary.
7. Reconfirm event dates and floor-plan URLs before deployment and after any schedule change.

Website checklist coverage
- Clear description of exhibit booth space and related event services
- Upcoming event dates and linked floor plans
- Sponsorship, advertising, registration and media-service descriptions
- USD currency disclosure
- Direct email, phone and physical business address
- Refund and cancellation policy
- Fulfillment and delivery policy
- Explicit limitation excluding product sales or revenue participation
- Privacy policy
- General terms
- Payment security language
- Accepted payment method indicators
- Domain-matched contact email

Important
The website, processor application, contracts, invoices and actual payment activity must remain consistent. Payment processors independently determine account eligibility and may request additional documentation.
